# Week 10 Patent Notes

## Project
SIM-003 MQTT Telemetry Simulator

## Purpose

The purpose of this research is to identify technical ideas that may have potential for further innovation in EV battery telemetry monitoring and cybersecurity testing.

## Potential Technical Concepts

### 1. Battery Telemetry Attack Simulation
A simulator can generate different abnormal telemetry conditions such as replay, spoofed ID, duplicate packets, delayed data, missing timestamps, and out-of-range values.

### 2. Replay Attack Detection
The system can compare telemetry timestamps and event information to identify previously generated data being replayed.

### 3. Battery Identity Validation
Battery identifiers can be validated against expected identifiers to identify possible spoofed telemetry sources.

### 4. Anomaly Flagging
Telemetry events can be automatically marked with anomaly information so that abnormal events can be easily identified during testing.

## Patent Consideration

These concepts are only preliminary research notes. Patentability cannot be determined from this project alone. A detailed prior-art search, technical novelty review, and patent evaluation would be required before making any patent claim.

## Future Improvement

Future work may include real MQTT broker integration, stronger authentication, digital signatures, persistent event history, and automated anomaly detection.

## Conclusion

The research identified several possible areas for improving EV battery telemetry cybersecurity and testing. Further research is required to determine technical novelty and patent potential.
